How our prices work

The price on every unit is what you pay before tax. Nothing mandatory gets added at the desk except HST and the government’s plate fee.

Included in every price

PDI, demo and starter kit: $2,995
Our pre-delivery inspection of the unit’s systems, a hands-on demonstration when you pick it up, and a starter kit.
Admin and licensing: $599
Preparing the paperwork and registering the sale.

An example

2016 Forest River Flyte 3150K
Unit$13,900
PDI, demo and starter kit$2,995
Admin and licensing$599
Advertised price$17,494
HST (13%)$2,274.22
Plates and permit (government fee)$72
Total if you take nothing optional$19,840.22

If you only need a permit (not plates), the government fee is $32.

Optional extras

You choose these. They are never part of the advertised price, and you can say no.

Extended warranty

$1,995 a year, for up to 5 years. Ask us which units qualify and what it covers.

Delivery

$6.50 per km, or pick it up here at no charge. Delivery details

Paying and deposits

Funds are held for 10 days to clear, and every unit is paid in full before ownership is transferred.

Want to hold a unit? Call us. Our written deposit terms are being finalized, and we’ll go over them with you before you pay anything.

Questions about a price?

Call the sales desk and we’ll go through any number with you.
